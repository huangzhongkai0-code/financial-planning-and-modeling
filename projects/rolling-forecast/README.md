# Revenue Forecast & Variance Review

How does a transparent forecast compare with reported sales?

## Role relevance
FP&A · Budgeting · Management reporting

## Data
Apple FY2023–FY2025; scenarios explicitly labelled. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm. Retrieved 2026-10-30.

## Method
Annual rolling-origin baseline: project next fiscal year revenue at 5% above the preceding year, then compare with FY2024 and FY2025 actuals. The 5% rate is an illustrative fixed budget assumption, not company guidance or a pre-registered forecast. FY2026 scenarios separately apply product growth of 0/3/6% and service growth of 5/10/15%.

## Findings
FY2025 reported revenue exceeded the illustrative budget by $5.57bn. FY2026 base-case revenue is $436.29bn.

- FY2025 actual $416.16bn
- Budget variance 1.36%
- Base scenario $436.29bn

## Interpretation and limits
Two annual comparisons are a worked FP&A example, not a statistically validated forecasting model. Annual public reports do not reveal internal monthly budgets, unit volumes or price changes. Scenario assumptions are analyst-created.

See [computed results](results.csv). Reproduce with `node analysis.mjs` from the repository root. This is an independent, AI-assisted portfolio case study, not employment experience or a company-issued forecast.
